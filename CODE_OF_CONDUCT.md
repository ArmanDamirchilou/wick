# Code of Conduct

This project is built by and for people who might be new to open source —
including the maintainer. The bar is simple: be someone people want to
collaborate with.

**Expected:**
- Assume good faith. Most confusing PRs and blunt comments aren't malicious.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Be patient with beginners. Everyone here is learning something.

**Not okay:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory comments or jokes.
- Publishing anyone's private information without consent.
- Deliberately derailing issues or PRs.

## Enforcement

If someone's behavior isn't okay, report it by opening a private issue or
emailing the maintainer directly (see the GitHub profile for contact info).
Reports will be handled discreetly. Maintainers may warn, edit, remove
contributions, or block anyone who violates this, at their discretion.

Loosely inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
